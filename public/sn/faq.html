Mibvunzo inowanzobvunzwa

Evenly imahara here?

Hongu. Evenly imahara zvachose uye icharamba iri mahara. Hapana kubhadhara, hapana zvishambadzo, hapana vhezheni yepremium.

Ndinoda account here?

Kwete. Hapana anoda account, kunyange anogadzira chikwata. Nyora zita, wedzera zvakabhadharwa, govera link.

Kugovana kunoshanda sei?

Pachinhu chimwe nechimwe chakabhadharwa unosarudza kuti ndiani akabhadhara uye kuti chinogovaniswa sei: zvakaenzana kune vese, zvakaenzana kune vamwe chete, huwandu chaihwo pamunhu, kana zvikamu (semuenzaniso 2:1:1). Masendi asingagoni kugovaniswa zvakaenzana anoenda kune akabhadhara, kuti zvese pamwe chete zvigare zvakaenzana chaizvo.

Ndiani anobhadhara ani?

Evenly inoverenga zvakabhadharwa nemunhu wese uye zvaanokwereta, yobva yaratidza mibhadharo mishoma inodiwa kuti vese vapedze kubhadharana. Kana mumwe abhadhara, baya "Nyora kuti zvabhadharwa" uye mabalance anovandudzwa kune vese.

Imhando dzemari nemitauro ipi zviripo?

Mhando dzemari dzinopfuura 60, imwe pachikwata, nemitauro 23, kusanganisira chiShona, Chirungu, French, Spanish, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Russian, Swahili, Tamazight neKurdish. Mutauro unotevera browser yako uye unogona kuchinjwa nebhatani renyika.

Ndingawedzera vanhu vasingashandisi Evenly ivo pachavo here?

Hongu. Sarudza "Wedzera nhengo" wonyora zita. Chero ani anogona kunyora zvakabhadharwa zvemumwe munhu; chikwata chinoona zvese.

Ndiani anogona kuona chikwata changu?

Chero ane link. Link icode refu risingafungidzirwi, uye mapeji ezvikwata haaiswi musearch engines. Govera link nechikwata chako chete.

Chii chinoitika kudata yangu?

Zita rechikwata, mazita enhengo, zvakabhadharwa nemibhadharo chete ndizvo zvinochengetwa. Hapana email, hapana nhamba yefoni, hapana ruzivo rwebhangi, hapana kutevera. Chero ane link anogona kudzima chikwata zvachose, uye zvese zvinobva zvaenda zvachose. Kana pasina anodzima, chinogara mwedzi gumi nemiviri mushure mechiitiko chekupedzisira kuti ugone kudzoka, chobva chadzimwa choga.
